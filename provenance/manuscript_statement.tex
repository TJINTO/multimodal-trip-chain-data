A source-level provenance registry is available at \url{https://github.com/TJINTO/multimodal-trip-chain-data/blob/main/metadata.csv}. It records source URLs, platforms, time-zone-qualified capture timestamps, SHA-256 hashes of successfully extracted text, snapshot types, and documented uses. Structured-data artifacts are linked through a separate file-hash inventory. Licensed encyclopedia text snapshots are provided at immutable Git commit URLs; for other material, capture receipts and hashes are shared without redistributing full text. New audit captures are distinguished from historical acquisition records, and unknown historical timestamps remain unspecified.
